## Overview

NVIDIA, a leading manufacturer of graphics processing units (GPUs), has recently reported that it is discontinuing the RTX 5090 and GB202 GPUs. This decision is being attributed to a significant shift in the company's product roadmap. Instead of focusing on the RTX 5090, which is primarily aimed at high-end gaming and professional graphics, NVIDIA is now planning to reserve these GPUs exclusively for the RTX PRO series.

The discontinuation of the RTX 5090 and GB202 GPUs marks a significant change in NVIDIA's product strategy, indicating a move towards a more focused approach on the RTX PRO lineup. This move is expected to be a catalyst for the upcoming RTX 3090 and GB204 GPUs, which are specifically designed for gaming, professional workloads, and high-performance computing (HPC) needs.

This change underscores the importance of product differentiation and the strategic importance of maintaining a clear product roadmap that aligns with market trends and consumer demand. As a result, manufacturers and consumers alike need to be aware of these shifts and make necessary adjustments to their purchasing and usage strategies.

## Key Features

The RTX 5090 and RTX GB202 GPUs are designed for high-end gaming and professional graphics applications. They offer exceptional performance in DirectX 12 and DirectX 11 games, surpassing the capabilities of the RTX 3090. These GPUs are equipped with cutting-edge RTX technology, which enables ray tracing, improved AI performance, and enhanced visual effects, including improved shadows and reflections. Additionally, they feature high performance for professional applications like video editing, 3D modeling, and rendering, making them ideal for professionals and enthusiasts alike.

## Use Cases

In a professional setting, the RTX 5090 and RTX GB202 GPUs are crucial for complex simulations and renderings in fields such as engineering, architecture, and film production.
